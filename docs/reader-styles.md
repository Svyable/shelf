## Publication recommendation, reader control

A publication can recommend type, paper, font size, tracking, leading, measure, alignment, paragraph rhythm, indentation, Pages or Continuous mode, hyphenation, and atmosphere through its `reader.json`.

Those values are a starting composition. A reader’s personal changes stay in that browser and never rewrite the publication.
