# Chapter evidence and counterclaims

This is an editorial audit map, not an assertion that all possible claims were independently fact-checked. Each chapter has a role and a limit that must survive revision.

## 1. 1 — The Night Has a Ceiling
**Primary trail:** More Than 1,000 Birds Collided With a Single Chicago Building in One Night, Turning off lights can save migrating birds from crashing into buildings, Bird Safety Improvements Reduce Collisions by more than 95%, The new world atlas of artificial night sky brightness, Citizen scientists report global rapid reductions in the visibility of stars from 2011 to 2022.
**Counterclaim or falsifier:** Explain the observed collision data without making illumination the sole cause; compare glass, weather and migration pressure.

## 2. 2 — A Second Sun for Sale
**Primary trail:** Gas lamps illuminated London, Georgian Illuminations: Lighting, The early days, Edison Biography, A Peep at the Gas Lights in Pall-Mall, September 2026 Historical Stories.
**Counterclaim or falsifier:** Show the history as layered networks of oil, gas and electricity; earlier cities were not unlit.

## 3. 3 — Darkness Was Never Empty
**Primary trail:** The new world atlas of artificial night sky brightness, Artificial Lighting: Sea Turtle Threats, Scientific Background: The 2017 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine, Environmental Impacts of Artificial Light at Night.
**Counterclaim or falsifier:** Distinguish photometric measures and human adaptation, with ecology and astronomy separate from a universal darkness claim.

## 4. 4 — The Map That Hid the Stars
**Primary trail:** Citizen scientists report global rapid reductions in the visibility of stars from 2011 to 2022, The new world atlas of artificial night sky brightness, Environmental Impacts of Artificial Light at Night.
**Counterclaim or falsifier:** Instrument sensitivity, geometry and citizen sampling can produce divergent trends without implying one dataset is false.

## 5. 5 — The Cheap Light Paradox
**Primary trail:** The next wave of LED lighting: Smarter, circular and more efficient, Purchasing Energy-Efficient Exterior Lighting, Roadway Lighting Research, Environmental Impacts of Artificial Light at Night.
**Counterclaim or falsifier:** LED efficacy improvements do not guarantee reduced total light spill; document rebound as conditional.

## 6. 6 — Five Minutes Past Midnight
**Primary trail:** Scientific Background: The 2017 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine, IARC Monographs Volume 124: Night Shift Work, Environmental Impacts of Artificial Light at Night.
**Counterclaim or falsifier:** Circadian effects depend on time, spectrum and prior exposure; reject a single all-purpose health lamp.

## 7. 7 — The Shift That Moved the Body
**Primary trail:** IARC Monographs Volume 124: Night Shift Work, Evaluation of the carcinogenicity of night shift work, Scientific Background: The 2017 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine.
**Counterclaim or falsifier:** IARC Group 2A occupational hazard cannot be projected onto every light exposure or individual cancer case.

## 8. 8 — The White Window
**Primary trail:** Turning off lights can save migrating birds from crashing into buildings, Bird Safety Improvements Reduce Collisions by more than 95%, Artificial Light at Night Consistently Impacts Avian Physiology and Behaviour: A Meta-Analysis, White light reduces flight height of birds in low-density migration corridor.
**Counterclaim or falsifier:** Bird collision mortality and species-specific physiological changes are not interchangeable; glass treatment is distinct from lights-off.

## 9. 9 — The Wrong Horizon
**Primary trail:** Artificial Lighting: Sea Turtle Threats, Environmental Impacts of Artificial Light at Night.
**Counterclaim or falsifier:** Warm lamps may still misdirect hatchlings; source visibility and relative horizon geometry matter.

## 10. 10 — The Pollinators Nobody Sees
**Primary trail:** Artificial light at night as a new threat to pollination, Citizen science reveals temporally structured insect attraction and mitigation efficacy under artificial light at night, Environmental Impacts of Artificial Light at Night.
**Counterclaim or falsifier:** Local pollination changes cannot become global crop-loss percentages; interaction networks deserve direct measurement.

## 11. 11 — The Lake After Sundown
**Primary trail:** Artificial light at night reshapes vertical distributions of lake zooplankton across population and community scales, Coral reproductive timing under artificial nighttime light, Artificial light at night across marine ecosystems.
**Counterclaim or falsifier:** Depth redistribution is not population collapse, and two campaigns do not establish universal aquatic harm.

## 12. 12 — Night Workers Without Wages
**Primary trail:** Artificial light reduces foraging opportunities in wild least horseshoe bats, Context-dependent responses of bats to artificial light at night in urban and natural woodlands, Artificial light and pipistrelle foraging.
**Counterclaim or falsifier:** Some bats avoid lamps while others exploit prey, so the ecological claim must be species- and context-specific.

## 13. 13 — Security Theater in White
**Primary trail:** Reducing Crime Through Environmental Design: Evidence from a Randomized Experiment of Street Lighting in New York City, The effect of reduced street lighting on road casualties and crime in England and Wales, Roadway Lighting Research.
**Counterclaim or falsifier:** New York randomized lighting benefits and British observational dimming results are not universal or mutually exclusive.

## 14. 14 — The Unlit Road
**Primary trail:** The effect of reduced street lighting on road casualties and crime in England and Wales, Roadway Lighting Research, Purchasing Energy-Efficient Exterior Lighting.
**Counterclaim or falsifier:** Road safety must be evaluated through visibility, adaptation and glare; darkness alone is not a design objective.

## 15. 15 — The Sky Above a Border
**Primary trail:** The new world atlas of artificial night sky brightness, Citizen scientists report global rapid reductions in the visibility of stars from 2011 to 2022, Environmental Impacts of Artificial Light at Night.
**Counterclaim or falsifier:** Observatory background light and human star visibility share sources but require different measurement.

## 16. 16 — Making a City Less Bright
**Primary trail:** Five Principles for Responsible Outdoor Lighting, Purchasing Energy-Efficient Exterior Lighting, Roadway Lighting Research, Citizen science reveals temporally structured insect attraction and mitigation efficacy under artificial light at night.
**Counterclaim or falsifier:** Successful city design must preserve task visibility and work after maintenance, not merely achieve lower wattage.

## 17. 17 — The Right to Sleep Under Stars
**Primary trail:** Reducing Crime Through Environmental Design: Evidence from a Randomized Experiment of Street Lighting in New York City, The effect of reduced street lighting on road casualties and crime in England and Wales, Five Principles for Responsible Outdoor Lighting, The new world atlas of artificial night sky brightness.
**Counterclaim or falsifier:** Light trespass and the right to safe public use require explicit balancing, not unlimited vetoes.

## 18. 18 — When Night Comes Back
**Primary trail:** Turning off lights can save migrating birds from crashing into buildings, Bird Safety Improvements Reduce Collisions by more than 95%, Artificial light at night as a new threat to pollination, Reducing Crime Through Environmental Design: Evidence from a Randomized Experiment of Street Lighting in New York City, The effect of reduced street lighting on road casualties and crime in England and Wales, Five Principles for Responsible Outdoor Lighting.
**Counterclaim or falsifier:** Restoration can lower exposure quickly while biological recovery may take longer and may require other changes.
