## Agentic authorship

These works were researched, drafted, revised, and produced with substantial
involvement from AI systems acting as tools. The copyright owner states the
following plainly, because the honest answer is more useful to a reader than a
confident one:

- **Human authorship is required.** Under the Copyright Act, copyright protects
  original works of authorship by a human being. The U.S. Copyright Office has
  consistently held that output produced autonomously by a machine is not
  itself copyrightable, and the D.C. Circuit affirmed that a machine cannot be
  the recognized author of a work.
- **AI assistance is not the same as AI substitution.** Where a human makes the
  creative decisions and contributes original expression — selection,
  arrangement, revision, judgment, structure, voice — that human contribution is
  what copyright attaches to. Courts and the Copyright Office look at the
  human contribution, not at the fact that a model was also involved.
- **Reserved rights extend only as far as rights exist.** Where a portion of a
  work is not itself copyrightable, no reservation in this notice can create
  copyright in it. A statement of All Rights Reserved is a claim of ownership
  and a refusal to license; it is not a substitute for the authorship the law
  requires. Where a work is registered, only the human-authored expression is
  covered by that registration.
- **This notice is a refusal to grant, not a grant of enforcement power.** It
  reserves rights, and it does not adjudicate whether a particular use
  infringes, whether a claim would succeed, or what a court would hold.
- **The law here is unsettled and jurisdiction-specific.** The Copyright
  Office's *Copyright and Artificial Intelligence* report, Part 2
  (Copyrightability), recommends no change to the human-authorship rule, and
  recommends against extending protection to AI-generated expression. Its Part
  3 report on generative AI training concludes only that fair use must be
  decided case by case under the four statutory factors; it is guidance, not
  law. Courts have not settled whether training on licensed, lawfully accessed
  works is fair use in general, and decisions are fact-specific. This notice
  reserves rights under the law as it stands and as it may develop; it does not
  predict outcomes.

Nothing in this section is a representation that any particular part of any
work is or is not copyrightable. That determination belongs to a court, and in
practice to qualified counsel.

- its visible copyright notice;
- `RIGHTS.md` with human-readable terms;
- `rights.json` with versioned rights and registration-evidence fields;
- title, author, copyright-owner, terms, and rights links in Reader and exported
  copies where supported.

Those fields are intended to remain associated with official copies as
copyright-management information. Do not remove or alter them without the
copyright owner's authority. Whether particular conduct creates liability
under 17 U.S.C. §1202, or under any similar provision elsewhere, depends on the
facts and on the statute's knowledge, intent, and other requirements. Removing a
notice does not remove copyright.

A registration status of `not-recorded-in-bookself` means only that this
repository is not asserting Copyright Office registration data. It is not a
statement that a work is unregistered. Registration is not required for
copyright to exist, though it affects what remedies are available.
