# CHORD — Research and source method

**Evidence cutoff:** November 16, 2026. **Scope:** neural representation and report, recurrent temporal binding, global access, thalamocortical gating, perturbational measures, agency, computational continuity and AI-consciousness indicators.

This book is evidence-driven investigative nonfiction, not a claim of a discovered consciousness algorithm. It separates (1) peer-reviewed primary experiments and clinical modeling, (2) preprints and theoretical research, (3) unpublished TACH synthetic experiments, and (4) proposed CHORD mechanisms and tests.

## Reader promise and distinct contribution

The important question is not whether one can build a machine that describes awareness. It is whether a restricted-information system can coordinate local inference, selective global availability, recurrent binding, and durable action-conditioned world models better than competent alternatives at equal information and compute. The book traces the hypothesis through its own falsifying results rather than presenting only neural analogies or optimistic architecture diagrams.

Its strongest competing explanation is ordinary state estimation plus adaptive communication: generic recurrent networks, Bayesian filters, symbolic causal planners, and task-aware active sensing may supply the same functions without privileged micro-epochs or a central conscious workspace. These rivals must be allowed to win.

## The proposed CHORD program

**Priority 1 — Geometry and causal remapping:** improve identity learning first; use matched B/C/D controls; measure Jacobian or cross-decoding changes; targeted equal-energy perturbation at learned event times. Reject remapping necessity if it can be disabled without selective loss or generic recurrence matches performance.

**Priority 2 — Recurrent binding deadlines:** conserve external samples; vary interruption relative to local integration completion; compare feature-conjunction and isolated detection; match generic recurrent and adaptive-compute controls. Reject a special deadline module if ordinary recurrence suffices at equal cost.

**Priority 3 — Readiness and selection dissociation:** independently perturb broad processing responsiveness and content-specific access under a factorial design; compare the effect to generic compute reduction. Do not map software scalar values onto anatomical thalamic nuclei.

**Priority 4 — Causal continuity:** test occluded objects, delayed actions, changing actuator regimes, and diagnostic information costs in paired worlds. Compare Bayesian, symbolic, recurrent and CHORD world models.

**Priority 5 — Perturbational evidence:** treat complexity as a secondary descriptor tied to task-causal effects and negative controls, not as an artificial consciousness certificate.

## Falsification and authoring rules

For every experiment, record identical observations, actual content bandwidth and side channels, model parameters and FLOPs, state memory, input timestamps, action deadlines, training budget, and held-out conditions. Report paired seed/world distributions, confidence intervals, failures, and exact protocol dates. Label task-performance gains as such; do not infer phenomenal experience.
