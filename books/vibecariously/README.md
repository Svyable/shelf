## Premise

Generative AI changes imagination less by replacing an internal human faculty than by collapsing the distance between an internal possibility and an external representation. Ideas can become visible, conversational, revisable, and emotionally inhabitable before we know whether we believe in them. The resulting abundance shifts creative scarcity toward taste, direction, judgment, surprise, embodied skill, verification, and commitment.

The book calls one emerging behavior *vibecarious*: experiencing the feel of a possibility through generated media without paying the full cost of inhabiting the underlying reality.
