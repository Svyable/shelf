# Chapter research map and falsification notes

Each chapter is anchored to primary literature or a disclosed original simulation. The source ledger records the exact version and evidence status. The following are the prepublication editorial commitments, not completed experiments.

## 1. The Image That Never Left
**Evidence:** Rapid alternation of conscious and suppressed stimulus representations in the primate brain, Adversarial testing of global neuronal workspace and integrated information theories of consciousness, Rapid concerted switching of the neural code in the inferotemporal cortex.
**Falsifier/limit:** Local decoding with absent report is not sufficient to locate experience; no-report measurement must control decision and task effects.

## 2. One Brain, Several Clocks
**Evidence:** The distinct modes of vision offered by feedforward and recurrent processing, Timing of the brain events underlying access to consciousness during the attentional blink, The threshold for conscious report: Signal loss and response bias in visual and frontal cortex.
**Falsifier/limit:** If irregular recurrence and feedforward alternatives match under fixed inputs and compute, a privileged processing clock is unsupported.

## 3. Twenty Milliseconds
**Evidence:** Rapid concerted switching of the neural code in the inferotemporal cortex, TACH-6 corrected eight-seed remapping experiment.
**Falsifier/limit:** A fixed or generic recurrent code that matches identity and category performance removes the case for a dedicated code switch.

## 4. The Binding Deadline
**Evidence:** Neural limits of temporal feature binding in human visual cortex, Rapid concerted switching of the neural code in the inferotemporal cortex.
**Falsifier/limit:** If perturbing recurrent integration does not selectively harm feature conjunctions, the binding-deadline mechanism is unsupported.

## 5. Fifteen Hertz Is Not a Theory
**Evidence:** Rapid alternation of conscious and suppressed stimulus representations in the primate brain, Rapid concerted switching of the neural code in the inferotemporal cortex, A Mind Cannot Be Smeared Across Time.
**Falsifier/limit:** A phase effect disappears when samples and processing budgets are conserved: reject intrinsic periodicity on that task.

## 6. Who Gets the Room?
**Evidence:** Adversarial testing of global neuronal workspace and integrated information theories of consciousness, The threshold for conscious report: Signal loss and response bias in visual and frontal cortex, Timing of the brain events underlying access to consciousness during the attentional blink.
**Falsifier/limit:** A matched distributed or targeted-messaging system equals global broadcast: do not call a central workspace necessary.

## 7. Two Kinds of Permission
**Evidence:** Targeting central thalamus with transcranial ultrasound reduces arousal in healthy volunteers, Individualised electrophysiological neural field models for the assessment of thalamocortical function in disorders of consciousness: A multicentre study.
**Falsifier/limit:** Readiness and selection perturbations fail to yield distinguishable impairments: collapse the proposed double gate.

## 8. How Far Can a Hidden Thought Go?
**Evidence:** Is it possible to make unconscious inferences? The limits of the influence of unconsciously perceived stimuli on conscious decisions, Adversarial testing of global neuronal workspace and integrated information theories of consciousness.
**Falsifier/limit:** Local-only model with equal information solves the entire task ladder: no unique global inference advantage.

## 9. Knock, Then Listen
**Evidence:** Breakdown of cortical effective connectivity during sleep, A theoretically based index of consciousness independent of sensory processing and behavior, Stratification of unresponsive patients by an independently validated index of brain complexity.
**Falsifier/limit:** Complexity rises independently of integration and task competence: complexity is not a standalone consciousness indicator.

## 10. Patients Are Not Training Labels
**Evidence:** Individualised electrophysiological neural field models for the assessment of thalamocortical function in disorders of consciousness: A multicentre study, Stratification of unresponsive patients by an independently validated index of brain complexity, A theoretically based index of consciousness independent of sensory processing and behavior.
**Falsifier/limit:** Model-derived thalamocortical parameters fail to predict independent behavior or intervention outcomes: avoid mechanistic overreach.

## 11. Local Weather, Global Decisions
**Evidence:** TACH-0 through TACH-2 synthetic access and recurrence studies, The distinct modes of vision offered by feedforward and recurrent processing.
**Falsifier/limit:** Separately trained one-way recurrence matches two-way control: reciprocal feedback not required in tested task.

## 12. Who Moved the World?
**Evidence:** TACH-3 and TACH-4 causal attribution and active sensing, TACH-0 through TACH-2 synthetic access and recurrence studies.
**Falsifier/limit:** A competent Bayesian causal estimator matches the proposed agent: no special self/world architecture demonstrated.

## 13. The Gate That Loved Noise
**Evidence:** TACH-5 reliability-aware content promotion, TACH-3 and TACH-4 causal attribution and active sensing.
**Falsifier/limit:** Noise-normalized access gains vanish against stronger estimation baselines or under held-out reliability regimes.

## 14. The Switch That Did Not Matter
**Evidence:** TACH-6 corrected eight-seed remapping experiment, Rapid concerted switching of the neural code in the inferotemporal cortex, Rapid alternation of conscious and suppressed stimulus representations in the primate brain.
**Falsifier/limit:** No selective impairment under remapping ablation and wide comparative uncertainty: no causal remapping advantage.

## 15. Fair Clocks
**Evidence:** TACH-5 reliability-aware content promotion, TACH-6 corrected eight-seed remapping experiment, Neural limits of temporal feature binding in human visual cortex, Adversarial testing of global neuronal workspace and integrated information theories of consciousness.
**Falsifier/limit:** Matching observations, latency, compute and budgets eliminates phase benefits: reject privileged timing necessity.

## 16. CHORD
**Evidence:** Rapid concerted switching of the neural code in the inferotemporal cortex, Neural limits of temporal feature binding in human visual cortex, Targeting central thalamus with transcranial ultrasound reduces arousal in healthy volunteers, TACH-6 corrected eight-seed remapping experiment, TACH-3 and TACH-4 causal attribution and active sensing.

## 17. Where the Theory Breaks
**Evidence:** TACH-5 reliability-aware content promotion, TACH-6 corrected eight-seed remapping experiment, Adversarial testing of global neuronal workspace and integrated information theories of consciousness, Identifying indicators of consciousness in AI systems.
**Falsifier/limit:** Any positive outcome can be post-hoc renamed to fit: the hypothesis has ceased to be falsifiable.

## 18. Nobody Has to Believe the Machine
**Evidence:** Consciousness in Artificial Intelligence: Insights from the Science of Consciousness, Identifying indicators of consciousness in AI systems, A Mind Cannot Be Smeared Across Time, Rapid alternation of conscious and suppressed stimulus representations in the primate brain.
**Falsifier/limit:** First-person claims or theory-indicator scores without independent validation do not establish subjective experience.
